## Design decisions

- **Cold ground, warm photographs.** Snow-shadow lavender-grey so the chestnut and black
  horses supply all the warmth. Deliberately not the warm-cream-and-terracotta look.
- **Their own violet.** The orchid in their logo is the single accent; nothing else competes.
- **Case-file structure.** The rescue exists because of law-enforcement seizures, so the
  page is built like an intake record: monospace labels, dated entries, real weights.
- **Georgia against monospace.** Editorial gravity for the headlines, a records voice for
  the labels. Both universally available, so no font can silently fail to load.
- **The zero.** The strongest true fact on their site is that no horse is currently waiting
  for a home. That gets a full-bleed inverted band instead of the usual urgency appeal.
- Light and dark themes both defined at token level; motion respects reduced-motion.

## Honest notes

- All action buttons (donate, volunteer, foster, adopt) point at the real site's pages.
  Nothing on the concept collects money or details.
- Only two horse photographs exist on their site, so Kat's record shows "no photograph on
  file" rather than a stand-in. A real build would want a proper photo shoot.
