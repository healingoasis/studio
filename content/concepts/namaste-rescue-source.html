Equine rescue · Wisconsin · Since 2014

When the law takes a horse away, we take him next.

Namaste Equine Rescue receives equines seized by law enforcement across Wisconsin. We put the weight back on, sort out the feet, rebuild the trust — and only then do we look for the home that keeps them.

“To procure equines seized by law enforcement, primarily serving the state of Wisconsin, to rehabilitate them mentally and physically and re-home them in a safe, forever environment.”

Our mission, word for word

We started in 2014 to help law enforcement handle seized horses. It took almost no time to learn that 99% of our purpose was educating owners — that a seizure works best when it ends with someone knowing what a horse actually needs.

So we do both. We take the horse. And where we can, we teach the person, so the next horse never has to be taken at all.

What actually happens

Three stages, and the middle one takes the longest.

A horse arrives on the worst day of his life. Everything after that is patience.

Stage 01

Seizure

Wisconsin law enforcement seizes an equine. We take custody, and the horse comes here — whatever condition he arrives in.

Stage 02

Rehabilitation

Shelter, feed, veterinary and farrier care, and unconditional attention. Weight comes back in months. Trust can take longer. Captain needed well over a year.

Stage 03

Rehoming

A horse is offered for adoption only once he is ready in body and mind. Never before that, however long it takes.
