## Getting signups, not just likes

1. Put the registration link **in the post**, not the first comment. Facebook shows link
   posts to slightly fewer people, but this is a conversion post — the click matters more
   than the reach.
2. **Boost it to a custom audience**: veterinarians, vet techs, chiropractors, within a few
   hours' drive of Chicago. A small budget aimed narrowly beats a big one aimed wide.
3. **Post it to the alumni group too**, with the 15% alumni discount as the lead. Alumni
   are the warmest audience you have.
4. **Answer every comment quickly** — questions about CE credit and the virtual option are
   the ones that turn into registrations.
5. The **live stream option** is the strongest line in the whole post. Anyone who wanted to
   come but can't travel is a sale you would otherwise lose. Say it every time.
