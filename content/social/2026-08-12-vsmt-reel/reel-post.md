# VSMT reel — 20 seconds, built for reach

**Video:** `vsmt-reel-20s.mp4` — 1080 x 1920, 30fps, exactly 20.00 seconds, 23 MB.

**No sound on purpose.** Add trending audio inside the app when you post. Reels with
platform audio get pushed harder than reels with none, and it keeps you clear of
copyright trouble.

## Why this version is built the way it is

The first cut was a brochure: five slow shots and a list of facts. This one follows the
shape reels actually travel in.

- **A hook in the first second, before any branding.** "She's not petting him. She's
  reading his spine." — a reframe on a close-up of hands on a horse. No logo, no title
  card. Anything that looks like an ad in second one gets scrolled past.
- **Twelve cuts instead of five.** Roughly 1.3 seconds each. Every cut is a reason to
  keep watching.
- **A slow push or pull on every shot**, alternating direction, so nothing sits still.
- **One idea per card, in plain words.** Short enough to read in a second.
- **Branding only at the end**, once someone has already watched.
- **Facts arranged as escalating proof**, ending on scarcity: 20 seats, that's the whole class.

## Getting the most reach out of it

Views come from the platform, not the edit. These are the levers that actually move it:

1. **Post it as a Reel**, not a regular video post, and share to Instagram as well.
2. **Add trending audio in-app.** Pick something with a steady beat, not a talking track.
3. **Don't put anything in front of the hook.** No intro card, no logo.
4. **Answer every comment in the first hour.** Early engagement is what decides how far
   it travels.
5. **Don't edit the caption after posting** — it can stall distribution.
6. **Give it 48 hours before judging it.** If it is still climbing, put a small boost
   behind it aimed at veterinarians and chiropractors.

## Rebuilding it

`overlays2.py` draws the text cards, `compose2.swift` cuts the clips together and exports
the MP4. Clip list, timings and wording are all at the top of each file.
