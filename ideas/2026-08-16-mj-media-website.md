## What it is

MJ Media, LLC (mjmedia.rocks) is the small South Milwaukee design and print shop —
websites, logos, printed material, banners and yard signs. Their current site is an
old WordPress theme. Daniel asked for a current, modern version: clean and confident,
not flashy or over the top.

Everything on the new site comes from their real, existing site: the services, the
prices, the schools they print signs for, the map towel, the team paragraph, and the
contact details (call Jim at (414) 651-2221, art@mediamj.com).

## Who it helps

MJ Media, and the small businesses, schools and non-profits around South Milwaukee
who are trying to figure out whether these are the right people to call.

## What "working" looks like

Someone lands on the page, understands in five seconds what MJ Media does, sees real
work they've printed, and either calls or sends a quote request without hunting.

## Open questions
