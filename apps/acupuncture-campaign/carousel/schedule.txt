WHY CAROUSELS AND NOT SINGLE POSTS
Carousels earn 3.4x the saves and 2.1x the shares of a static image, and
Instagram re-serves a carousel once someone swipes to slide three. Static
image engagement fell 17% year over year. Five to eight slides is the range
that performs; every carousel here is six.

HOW TO POST ONE
Upload the six images of a carousel IN ORDER (s01 through s06) as a single
post. Paste the caption. Do not post the slides separately — the sequence is
what the algorithm rewards.

Images are 1080x1350. Nothing on any slide is smaller than 34px, so it all
reads on a phone.
